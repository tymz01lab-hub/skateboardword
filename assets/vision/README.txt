MediaPipe Tasks Vision 0.10.32 and Pose Landmarker Lite

These existing runtime dependencies are served with the exhibition to avoid
third-party CDN availability differences between exhibition computers.
vision_bundle.js is the unmodified upstream vision_bundle.mjs (ES module).
Original download URLs and SHA-256 hashes are listed in sources.json.
The upstream license is preserved in LICENSE.

Pose inference runs locally inside a worker with the CPU/WASM delegate.
No camera frames are sent to a server.
